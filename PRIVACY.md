# Privacy

## English

lightstop processes camera frames and motion-sensor samples locally to calculate
exposure and track Zone markers. It does not request Internet, location, or
shared-storage permissions, does not contain an analytics or advertising SDK,
and does not transmit data to the developer or any third party.

The application does not take or save photographs. Preferences, camera labels,
meter calibration records, and vignetting calibration maps remain in the
application's private storage. Uninstalling the application normally removes
this local data according to Android's platform behavior.

The source code is publicly auditable. A distributor who modifies the project
by adding networking, analytics, crash reporting, cloud backup, or another data
processor must publish a privacy policy describing those changes.

## 中文

光档在设备本地处理相机帧和运动传感器采样，用于计算曝光并跟踪 Zone
标记点。应用不申请网络、定位或共享存储权限，不包含统计或广告 SDK，
不会向开发者或任何第三方传输数据。

不会拍摄保存照片。偏好设置、镜头备注、测光校准记录和暗角校准图
保存在应用私有存储中。按照 Android 平台的通常行为，卸载应用会移除这些
本地数据。

项目源代码可公开审计。若发行者修改项目并加入网络、统计、崩溃上报、
云备份或其他数据处理功能，应另外公布隐私政策并说明这些变化。
