# lightstop 0.6.0

## 中文

- 新增负片预览：一键识别与反相、片基采样、曝光/白平衡锁定，以及可增删控制点的 RGB 曲线。
- 手动选区支持拖动边、对角缩放和框外旋转；统一小工具、参数记录和设置的界面风格。
- 改进自动距离估算的兼容性、状态反馈与参数记录，恢复闪光设置更完整。
- 更新精简 OpenCV，保留实时处理需要的加速后端、三个 ABI 和第三方许可。

需要 Android 9 或以上。负片识别和自动距离仍受画面、镜头及设备影响，部分跨设备实机验证尚未完成。矩阵测光与连续负片分析的研究文档不代表已实现功能。

## English

- Added negative preview with one-tap detection and inversion, film-base sampling, exposure/white-balance locking, and editable RGB curve points.
- Crop by dragging edges, scaling from corners, or rotating outside the frame. Updated tool, record, and settings layouts.
- Improved automatic distance compatibility, feedback, saved distance, and flash-setting restoration.
- Updated the slim OpenCV runtime while retaining acceleration, three ABIs, and third-party notices.

Android 9 or newer. Detection and distance estimates depend on the scene, lens, and device; some device checks remain pending. Matrix-metering and continuous-analysis documents describe research, not shipped features.

## 发布检查 / Publishing
